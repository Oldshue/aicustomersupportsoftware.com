How we research and rank AI customer support software

Last updated: November 9, 2026

What we did

  • Built a shortlist of widely used AI customer support products: help desks with built-in AI agents, standalone AI agent platforms, and CRM-native agents.
  • Read each vendor's official product, pricing and documentation pages on November 9, 2026. Salesforce figures come straight from Salesforce's Agentforce and Service Cloud pricing pages. Ada's pricing page blocked automated access, so its pricing model comes from Ada's published customer terms. Gorgias's live pricing cards were not readable as text, so its prices come from Gorgias's own pricing guides and billing documentation.
  • Recorded only facts we could tie to a source: product names, channels, pricing models and published prices, and ownership changes such as Zendesk completing its acquisition of Forethought (March 26, 2026).

What we did not do

  • We did not test these products hands-on, run benchmarks or survey customers.
  • We did not treat vendor-reported resolution rates or customer quotes as independent evidence; any such claim is attributed to the vendor.
  • No vendor paid for placement, and the ranking does not use affiliate commissions.

How we ranked

The order is an editorial judgment, weighing:

  1. Support focus: how central AI customer service is to the product.
  2. Breadth: channels, ability to take actions in other systems, and handoff to humans.
  3. Fit for common buyers: SMB, mid-market, enterprise, ecommerce and CRM-centric teams.
  4. Pricing transparency: all else equal, published prices and clear billing units rank above undisclosed quotes.
  5. Ease of adoption: self-serve trials, and whether the product works alongside an existing help desk.

This is not a market-share or revenue ranking. A lower-ranked product can be the right choice for you; see 'How to choose' on the main page.

Updates and corrections

Pricing and packaging in this market change often. We re-check sources at each update and show the date at the top. Every entry links to the vendor's official page, which always takes precedence over this site.
