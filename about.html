About this site

Last updated: November 9, 2026

AICustomerSupportSoftware.com is an independent research site with one goal: to give a clear, current and honest answer to 'what is the best AI customer support software?'

What we are

  • An independent editorial site that turns public information, mainly vendors' official product, pricing and documentation pages, into a ranked guide.
  • Not affiliated with any vendor listed. No vendor reviewed or approved this content before publication.

What we are not

  • We are not a testing lab. We do not claim hands-on testing, benchmarks or customer reviews.
  • We do not publish invented reviewers, credentials or ratings.
  • We do not give legal, security or procurement advice. Confirm terms, pricing and compliance directly with vendors.

How to use this site

Start with the ranked list, compare costs on the pricing page, and read the methodology to see how the list was built. Every product links to its official site so you can check the details yourself.
