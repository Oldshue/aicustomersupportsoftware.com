How much does AI customer support software cost in 2026?

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Most AI customer support products now charge for results (resolutions, outcomes or conversations), and many also charge for help desk seats. This table lists only prices we found on vendors' own pages or documents. 'Not published' means the vendor quotes through sales. All prices are USD list prices and exclude taxes, discounts and implementation.

How to compare these prices

  • The units are not interchangeable. A 'resolution' usually means the AI closed the issue with no human handoff. Fin's 'outcome' also counts completed Procedures that end in a handoff. A Salesforce conversation or action is billed whatever the result.
  • Estimate volume and resolution rate. Monthly AI cost is roughly conversations × share the AI resolves × unit price, plus seats and add-ons.
  • Watch for minimums and overages. Examples: Fin's 50-outcome minimum on other help desks, Gorgias's overage tiers, Ada's purchased conversation allowance, and Salesforce billing anything above your Flex Credit entitlement monthly in arrears at your contracted rate.
